The problem

Rescue teams fly blind in the first minutes

During disasters, video streams overload weak networks, humans can't reach dangerous zones, and evacuees don't know where to go. AFRICA RESQ replaces guesswork with a machine-readable picture.

PAIN 01

Networks collapse

Live video needs bandwidth disaster zones don't have. We send a few bytes per event, not megabytes of video.

PAIN 02

Humans can't go in

Smoke, heat and rubble stop first responders. Our rover volunteers in the danger zone first — it's disposable and cheap.

PAIN 03

No shelter guidance

People don't know the nearest safe place. The API answers in one call: shelter, distance, walk & drive minutes, direction.

Extra winning point

Watch the system think, live

🏠 Nearest shelter

Real dataset, 13 cities. Haversine distance, direction, walk/drive estimates, city filter — sorted and returned in milliseconds.

📡 Events, not video

A detection is a few hundred bytes: type, confidence, GPS, bbox. Weak networks can carry it. OpenCV video stays optional on-site.

🔌 One API, zero polling

The SSE stream pushes new detections and state instantly. Dashboard code = ~20 lines of EventSource.
